Study plan to February 2027

Phase 1: build the base (now to mid Nov)

Cover Sections 1, 4, 5 and 2 first because they are calculation heavy. One topic at a time: read, make a one-page formula sheet, then solve 15 to 20 questions on it.

Phase 2: finish the syllabus (mid Nov to Dec)

Sections 3, 6, 7, 8 and 9. Many are conceptual, so use short notes and quick revision. Start topic-wise previous year questions in parallel.

Phase 3: mock tests (January)

Two full mocks a week in a 3-hour sitting. Spend as long analysing a mock as taking it. Keep an error log of every wrong or guessed question and its cause.

Phase 4: final two weeks

No new topics. Revise formula sheets and the error log, redo weak-section questions, and rehearse exam-day timing and the calculator-free mindset.

Habits that raise scores

  • Solve previous papers by topic first, then as full timed papers. Question styles repeat more than topics do.
  • Attempt easy questions in the first pass, mark doubtful ones, and return. Avoid guessing MCQs you cannot narrow down, since wrong answers cost marks.
  • Keep a single formula notebook per section and revise it weekly.
  • Log study hours and mock scores in the Tracker tab, and review the trend every Sunday.
  • Sleep and a fixed daily slot matter more than occasional long marathons.
